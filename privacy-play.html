About this policy

This policy explains how Kibanda Mobile (package allstream.heroh.labs), the app published by Heroh Labs UG on Google Play, handles information. It covers the Google Play version only. That version offers local Luganda movies and series, free to watch, with no account or sign-in.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, no payment and no profile.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information kept on your device

Your watch history and “Continue watching” progress are saved only on your phone so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us.

Movies and episodes you download are saved to your phone’s storage so you can watch them offline.

You can remove all of this at any time by deleting downloads in the app, clearing the app’s data in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.

Streaming

When you browse or play a title, the app connects to our servers and the hosting providers that deliver the video. Like any internet connection, those servers receive your IP address and basic technical details (such as the app version) so they can send the content to your device. We do not use this to identify you, build a profile or share it for marketing.

Permissions

  • Internet: to list and stream titles.
  • Storage: to save downloaded titles for offline viewing. The app only uses the files it downloads.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Children

Kibanda Mobile is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Connections between the app and our servers use encrypted HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date below.
